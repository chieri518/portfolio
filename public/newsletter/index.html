Kylie Wetzel '19

"Since January I’ve had to start dialysis and am waiting on a new kidney. This has been an immense change to both my body and my life as a whole. The current wait time on the national donor list is an average of 4-5 years. A living donor would make this process much shorter and allow me to return to “normal” life, happier and healthier." 

Menlo College alumna Kylie Wetzel '19 is looking for a new kidney. Kylie has persisted living with kidney problems over the last decade, but unfortunately was diagnosed with kidney failure after contracting COVID-19 in December 2022.

Introducing Two Masters Degrees!

A big announcement as Menlo College heads toward its second century: Introducing not one, but TWO, MASTERS programs!

"As an institution committed to academic excellence and meeting the needs of the modern workforce, we are delighted to launch these graduate programs [in Sport and Performance Psychology as well as Information Systems],” said President Steven Weiner. “Both fields are integral to today's professional landscape, and our students will gain a competitive edge through hands-on learning experiences in their profession and mentorship provided by our distinguished faculty.” 

“I am especially pleased that these programs provide options to our undergraduate students to continue their education at Menlo,” Weiner continued.

Menlo College's first graduate students will begin classes next month, at the start of the Fall 2023 semester.

Menlo Athletics Moving to NCAA DII

Menlo College has officially been accepted as a member of the NCAA's Division II! This first step initiates a three-year reclassification process, throughout which the NCAA tracks benchmarks to ensure a smooth transition.

"We will forever be grateful for our time in the NAIA, and today's decision marks a historic opportunity for Menlo College,"Vice President for Enrollment Management & Athletics Keith Spataro said. "We are excited about our future, which will include more regional rivalries [and] enhanced return on investment for our athletic program."

President Steven Weiner

Menlo College President Steven Weiner was recently elected to Joint Venture Silicon Valley's Board of Directors.

"I'm excited and honored to be appointed to the Board of Directors of Joint Venture Silicon Valley," President Weiner said. "Innovation is pivotal to Menlo College, and I look forward to the opportunity to collaborate with other area leaders as Joint Venture works to find innovative solutions to the challenging issues affecting Silicon Valley."
